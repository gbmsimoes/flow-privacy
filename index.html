FLOW Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

FLOW is published by Mondego Studios ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use FLOW.

Information we collect

FLOW does not require an account and does not directly collect your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, or payment information.

FLOW offers an optional Remove Ads purchase. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google through their store, and FLOW does not receive your payment details.

The game keeps basic gameplay events in memory during the current session, such as session duration, game-mode activity, scores, and gameplay actions. This information is used only within the game during that session and is not sent to Mondego Studios or to an analytics provider.

Advertising and consent

FLOW uses Google AdMob to provide optional rewarded advertisements and occasional interstitial advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, app and ad interactions, approximate location inferred from an IP address, and diagnostic information in order to serve, measure, secure, and improve ads. The information processed and the ads shown depend on your device, location, settings, and consent choices.

Where required, FLOW uses Google's User Messaging Platform to present a consent message before requesting ads. You can decline consent where that choice is offered. Google explains how it uses information collected when you use its partners' apps in its Privacy & Terms materials.

How information is used and shared

We use the advertising service to make optional ads available and to understand whether ads can be delivered safely and reliably. Advertising information is processed by Google and its advertising partners under their own policies. Mondego Studios does not sell personal information and does not receive a personal advertising profile from AdMob.

Your choices and privacy rights

You may choose the options presented in the consent message where it is shown. You can also reset or limit the advertising identifier through your Android or iOS device settings. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or information about the processing of your personal data. To make a request or ask a privacy question, contact us at mondegostudios@gmail.com.

Children's privacy

FLOW is intended for users 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through FLOW, please contact us so we can review the request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when FLOW or applicable requirements change. We will post the updated version here and change the date at the top of this page.
